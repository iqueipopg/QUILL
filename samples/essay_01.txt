Social media is bad for education. Students spend too much time on Instagram and TikTok instead of studying. This is a big problem.

First, social media is distracting. When students are trying to do homework they get notifications and then they stop working. A study showed that students who use social media a lot get lower grades.

Second, social media can cause cyberbullying. Students who are bullied online cant concentrate in school. This affects their grades and mental health.

In conclusion social media is negative for education because it distracts students and causes bullying.
