## Original essay

**Assignment prompt:** Essay about contacting with intelligent life forms on the other planets

> It is commonly believed that there are other shapes of existence outside our planet. While some researchers believe that it is a good idea to contact them, others think that it can potentially cause complications for people. 
> On the one hand, connecting to extraterrestrial beings means we will able to approach a completely new creature, so this activity is the opportunity to explore alien civilizations. If life exists in actual fact on another planet, then the data about their lifestyle, culture, history or language can be a valuable source that enriches human knowledge. Besides, those life forms might possess a large amount of information about the galaxy or the universe which humans have not acquired. This somehow helps us in finding a suitable place to inhabit in case the world's population grows too fast or uncontrollably. 
> On the other hand, some people believe that if other creatures learn of our existence, terrible scenarios will occur. Because of the uncertain details of alien beings, the language barrier seems to be the big problem. To be more specific, messages sent from Earth could have a different meaning for them, the misunderstanding can put the human in danger when they negatively interpret the messages. With advanced technology compared to what we got, all earth creatures will inevitably suffer casualties. 
> In conclusion, from my perspective, it is quite risky and dangerous if scientists decide to create a relationship with life on another planet but I believe the understanding from outside the place we live outweighs its negative side.
